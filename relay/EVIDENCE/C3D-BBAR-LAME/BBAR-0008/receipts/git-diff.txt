    fix(lafea3): reconcile coarse-mesh angular cell count in Lamé fixture
    
    Use nearest-integer subdivision rather than ceiling for the coarse
    non-probe angular interval in protectedAxis. This prevents artificial
    over-refinement from splitting [0, 23.33 deg] into two sliver cells
    and restores T6 L1 minimum corner angle from 11.198 deg to 17.23 deg,
    satisfying the mesh quality gate without BLOCK (threshold 11.537 deg).
