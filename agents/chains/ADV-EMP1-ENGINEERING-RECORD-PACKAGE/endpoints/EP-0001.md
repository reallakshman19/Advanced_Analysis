
## Review gate

Package creation requires a retained review record that is current against the exact supplied execution. Current `ACCEPTED` and current `REJECTED` reviews may both be packaged as truthful audit outcomes. `NOT_REVIEWED` and `REVIEW_STALE` fail closed.
